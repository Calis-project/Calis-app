# 02 — User Personas

## Primary Persona — The Restarting User

### Summary

This user often starts fitness routines but stops after a few days or weeks.

They are the most important user for the MVP.

### Goals

- build a realistic exercise habit
- avoid restarting from zero every time
- stop feeling guilty after breaks
- have a simple way to return

### Frustrations

- strict streaks feel punishing
- intense programs become overwhelming
- missing a week feels like failure
- the app does not adapt after breaks

### Product Response

Calis App should provide:

- Comeback Mode
- Return Chain
- minimum workouts
- supportive messages
- gentle restart sessions

### Example Message

> You came back. That matters more than the missed days.

## Persona 2 — The Busy Beginner

### Summary

This user has limited time and low confidence.

### Goals

- start exercising at home
- keep workouts short
- avoid complicated plans
- feel more energetic

### Frustrations

- workouts feel too long
- too many choices
- feels embarrassed about being a beginner

### Product Response

- three simple daily options
- short workouts
- beginner-friendly exercises
- no-shame language

## Persona 3 — The Apartment User

### Summary

This user lives in an apartment and needs quiet, small-space workouts.

### Goals

- avoid disturbing neighbors
- avoid jumping exercises
- exercise in limited space

### Frustrations

- many home workouts include jumps
- videos assume a large room
- no clear noise labels

### Product Response

- quiet mode
- no-jump exercises
- small-space filtering
- low-impact alternatives

## Persona 4 — The Low-Energy User

### Summary

This user often feels tired, stressed, or mentally overloaded.

### Goals

- move without pressure
- have very easy options
- keep the habit alive

### Frustrations

- standard workouts feel impossible on tired days
- exercise feels like another task
- apps assume high motivation

### Product Response

- 3-minute minimum workout
- mobility and recovery sessions
- calm messages
- energy check-in

## Persona 5 — The Cautious Mover

### Summary

This user wants to exercise but is careful with knees, back, wrists, or shoulders.

### Goals

- move safely
- use easier alternatives
- avoid painful movements

### Frustrations

- apps do not explain modifications
- hard exercises feel risky
- pain is not handled safely

### Product Response

- sensitive-area onboarding
- Plan B alternatives
- low-impact options
- safety notes
- stop-if-pain messaging

## Future AI Persona Support

AI may later support these personas by:

- adapting workouts to today’s energy
- creating personalized comeback messages
- explaining exercise alternatives
- summarizing progress supportively

But AI should not be required for the first MVP.
