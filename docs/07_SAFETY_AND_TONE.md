# 07 — Safety and Tone

## Safety Principle

Calis App is not a medical app.

It should not diagnose, treat, or prescribe.

## Required Safety Message

The app should include a basic safety disclaimer:

> This app provides general fitness guidance. Stop if you feel pain, dizziness, or unusual discomfort. If you have a medical condition, injury, or concern, consult a qualified healthcare professional before starting exercise.

## Exercise Safety Rules

The app should:

- prefer beginner-safe movements
- provide easier alternatives
- avoid high-impact exercises for comeback sessions
- respect sensitive areas
- suggest stopping if pain occurs
- include safety notes where needed

The app should not:

- tell users to push through pain
- make medical claims
- promise rapid results
- recommend extreme routines
- ignore user limitations

## Comeback Safety

Comeback sessions should be easier than normal sessions.

For longer breaks, use:

- mobility
- low-impact strength
- gentle full-body movement
- short duration

Avoid:

- high-intensity intervals
- jumping
- advanced movements
- long sessions

## Tone of Voice

The app should sound like a calm, supportive coach.

Tone should be:

- friendly
- realistic
- non-judgmental
- simple
- encouraging
- human

## Avoid These Phrases

- “No excuses”
- “You failed”
- “You lost your streak”
- “Go hard or go home”
- “Burn fat fast”
- “Crush your body”
- “Make up for missed days”
- “You are behind”

## Prefer These Phrases

- “Small progress counts.”
- “A short workout is still a workout.”
- “You came back. That matters.”
- “Today can be simple.”
- “Let’s restart gently.”
- “No need to make up for missed days.”
- “Choose the easier version if needed.”

## Message Examples

### Start

> What kind of day is it?

### Minimum Workout

> Low energy is okay. Let’s keep the habit alive with a short session.

### Completion

> Done. A short workout still counts.

### Comeback

> Welcome back. You are not starting from zero. Let’s restart gently.

### Progress

> You completed two short workouts this week. That is real progress.

## Future AI Tone Rules

AI-generated text must follow the same tone.

AI must not generate:

- shame-based messages
- medical claims
- aggressive fitness motivation
- body-shaming language
- unrealistic promises

AI messages should be short, supportive, and practical.
