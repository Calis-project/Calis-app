# 00 — Product Brief

## Product Name

**Calis App**

Working concept: a realistic home workout app focused on sustainable consistency.

## One-Sentence Summary

Calis App helps people exercise at home by making small workouts count and making it easy to return after missed days.

## Core Philosophy

> You do not need a perfect workout. You just need to come back.

## Problem

Many people start fitness apps with motivation but stop after a few days or weeks.

Common reasons:

- workouts feel too long
- users miss days and feel they failed
- streaks create guilt
- apps are too intense
- users have limited time, space, equipment, or energy
- fitness language can feel aggressive or unrealistic

## Product Opportunity

Most fitness apps are designed around starting strong.

Calis App should be designed around continuing realistically.

The key product difference is:

> The app treats returning after a break as progress.

## Target Users

Initial target users:

- beginners
- busy people
- people who repeatedly start and stop
- people exercising at home
- apartment users who need quiet workouts
- people who need low-pressure guidance

## Main Product Promise

The app should help users feel:

- I can start small.
- I do not need to be perfect.
- Missing a day does not destroy progress.
- I always have a simple way back.
- A short workout still counts.

## Product Positioning

Calis App is:

- realistic
- supportive
- home-based
- beginner-friendly
- low-pressure
- consistency-focused
- future-ready for optional AI support

Calis App is not:

- a hardcore transformation challenge
- a medical app
- a bodybuilding app
- a calorie-obsession app
- a shame-based streak tracker
- a generic AI chatbot

## Core Features

- Minimum Workout
- Daily Workout Options
- Comeback Mode
- Return Chain
- Home-Based Personalization
- Exercise Alternatives
- Supportive Progress Tracking

## AI Position

AI is planned as a future optional support layer.

AI is not part of the initial MVP.

The product must work without AI.
