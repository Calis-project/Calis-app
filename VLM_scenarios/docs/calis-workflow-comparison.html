What I'd ship

Let CV spend the VLM's budget, not the clock.

The failure mode isn't latency or token price — it's that every user shares one 1,500-call/day bucket. Any workflow whose call count scales with time (auto-snapshot) or with every set (naïve set-end) caps your free MVP at a handful of real users. The winning moves make CV decide when a cloud call is worth it and which frames it carries.

FREE MVP

Default: CV-gated Hybrid

CV counts every rep and scores form for free; Gemini fires only on the sets where form actually breaks (or reps get ambiguous). Same coaching value, a fraction of the calls — this is the design that keeps the most users inside the free tier.

FREE MVP

Cheapest tier: Batched end-of-workout

One call per session reviewing all sets with a keyframe each. Loses live nudges, but a single-user tester could train for weeks without touching the cap. Offer it as the "lite" plan.

GO PAID

Per-set + CV-guided keyframes

When you have paid quota, drop the gate and send rep-peak / worst-rep frames every set. CV-guided keyframes are where hybrid genuinely beats frames-only — CV improves the input, not just the prose.

LATER

Reverse hybrid (auto-calibration)

Post-set, let the VLM sanity-check CV's rep count/depth and nudge your per-user thresholds within caps. A few calls per calibration, never live — a v2 accuracy play, not an MVP cost concern.

Model & pricing notes. Free-tier limits per Google AI for Developers (2026): Gemini 3 Flash — 10 RPM, 250K TPM, 1,500 RPD; Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite — 15 RPM.
